
# AHIP Healthcare Management: An Introduction (AHM-250) Exam Guide

A dedicated, comprehensive study guide and healthcare management reference repository for candidates preparing for **AHIP Healthcare Management: An Introduction (AHM250)**, the course associated with the Professional, Academy for Healthcare Management (PAHM) designation.

---

## Exam Overview

AHIP's Healthcare Management: An Introduction course develops foundational knowledge of health insurance organizations, health plan operations, healthcare delivery, financing, and industry regulation. Successful completion of AHM250 earns the PAHM designation.

- **Exam Format**: Online, self-paced course with a course assessment; AHIP describes its designation programmes as including an exam for each course.
- **Target Audience**: Health Insurance Professionals, Agents and Brokers, Case Managers, Financial Planners, Medical Directors, and Health Plan Management Staff.
- **Prerequisites**: No formal prerequisites are stated in the available AHIP course information.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Evolution of Health Plans and Healthcare Delivery

- Development of healthcare delivery and financing in the United States, from prepaid plans to Accountable Care Organizations (ACOs).
- Evolution of managed care and the changing relationship between health plans, providers, and members.
- Healthcare industry trends, care models, and the role of health insurance organizations.

### 2. Basic Concepts of Health Insurance

- Health insurance principles, provider organizations, indemnity coverage, and managed care structures.
- Differences among Health Maintenance Organizations (HMOs), Preferred Provider Organizations (PPOs), Point-of-Service (POS) plans, and managed indemnity.
- Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), Health Reimbursement Arrangements (HRAs), risk, underwriting, and pricing.

### 3. Health Plan Benefits and Networks

- Health plan benefits, coverage structures, member cost-sharing, and provider network arrangements.
- Network structure, provider access, and network management in healthcare delivery.
- Health plan design and the relationship between coverage, services, and healthcare costs.

### 4. Provider Compensation and Healthcare Operations

- Provider compensation models, including fee-for-service and value-based care.
- Health insurance operations, claims administration, and health plan management principles.
- The role of information technology in supporting health plan and provider operations.

### 5. Healthcare Regulation and Government Programs

- Legislative and regulatory developments affecting health insurance, including the Affordable Care Act (ACA) and the 21st Century Cures Act.
- Medicare and Medicaid programmes and their roles in healthcare coverage.
- Industry terminology, emerging issues, and practical healthcare management scenarios.
